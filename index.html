Paolo Barucca, PhD

Associate Professor, Department of Computer Science, University College London

I work where statistical physics, network science and machine learning meet, building principled, physics-inspired models that give data-driven methods robustness, interpretability and solid theoretical foundations — especially in highly interconnected, non-stationary systems like financial markets.

Outside the lab I write and perform science comedy as La Scienza Coatta.
