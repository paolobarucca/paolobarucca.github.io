R&D

We develop statistical and machine learning models that must fit data while respecting constraints — liquidity or no-arbitrage constraints in portfolio construction, balance-sheet constraints in economic activity, physical constraints in spatial systems, and network constraints in socio-economic and financial systems.

Experiments

Interactive experiments we run in workshops, lectures and events. The room becomes the system and the model appears in front of everyone. Each one runs in the browser, with nothing to install.

Two-thirds of the average

Everyone picks a number, and the closest to two-thirds of the average wins. Play two rounds and see how many steps ahead the crowd reasons.

The 7 × 7 lattice

Forty-nine people, one spin each. Watch Ising, voter and Hopfield dynamics play out on the room itself.

Temporal network explorer

Fit and compare maximum-entropy temporal network models on synthetic data or your own edge lists, with diagnostics and animated dynamics.
